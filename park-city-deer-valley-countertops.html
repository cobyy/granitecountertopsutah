Custom Mountain Home Stone Applications

Mountain modern and alpine rustic homes in Park City, Deer Valley, Promontory, and Heber Valley demand large-scale stone work that goes well beyond standard perimeter counters:

Fireplace Surrounds & Hearths

Floor-to-ceiling bookmatched marble, quartzite, and leathered granite fireplace surrounds, custom mitered hearths, and solid stone mantles that anchor great rooms and master suites.

Mitered Waterfall Islands

Oversized chef's kitchen islands with 45-degree mitered waterfall legs where natural stone veining flows unbroken from the horizontal counter down to the floor.

Full-Height Stone Backsplashes

Continuous slab backsplashes behind professional 48-inch and 60-inch ranges, grain-matched directly to the countertop slab below with zero tile grout lines.

Spa Bathrooms & Steam Showers

Custom honed or polished marble and limestone vanity tops, integrated stone sinks, slab shower walls, benches, niches, and soaking tub decks.

Wet Bars, Wine Rooms & Ski Prep

Backlit translucent onyx bar tops, soapstone tasting counters, and durable granite or quartz mudroom and ski-tuning benches built for heavy winter gear.

High-Altitude Outdoor Kitchens

UV-stable natural granite and large-format porcelain slabs engineered to withstand 7,000+ ft elevation sun and Summit County freeze-thaw winters.

Most Requested Stones for Park City & Deer Valley Residences

  • Taj Mahal, Sea Pearl & Super White Quartzite: Translucent, marble-like movement with Mohs 7 hardness that stands up to large gatherings and entertaining.
  • Honed Calacatta, Statuario & Carrara Marble: Authentic Italian marble with a velvety matte honed finish for statement islands, baking stations, and master bath suites.
  • Leathered & Honed Granite: Tactile, low-glare finishes on Titanium, Black Cosmic, and Absolute Black granite slabs that complement timber beams, steel accents, and white oak cabinetry.
  • Engineered Quartz & Sintered Porcelain: Non-porous, zero-maintenance surfaces ideal for secondary guest suites, bunk rooms, laundry rooms, and luxury vacation rentals.

Private Slab Selection in Salt Lake City + Reliable Mountain Delivery.
