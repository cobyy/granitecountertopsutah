Countertop FAQ — Granite, Marble, Quartzite & Quartz in Utah

Straightforward answers about stone selection, pricing, slab yard visits, installation timelines, and care from master fabricator Howard Gerstner.

Countertop pricing in Utah typically ranges from $40 to $100+ per square foot installed, depending on the stone type (granite, marble, quartzite, or quartz), slab thickness, edge profile complexity, and the layout of your kitchen. We offer free in-home estimates so you know your exact cost before committing to anything.

The process has two main steps: first, Howard visits your home to take a precise template of your space. Then, we fabricate your countertop in-house and return to install it. From template to installation, most projects are completed within 1–2 weeks. The installation day itself usually takes just a few hours for a standard kitchen.

Granite is a 100% natural stone quarried from the earth. Every slab is unique, highly scratch-resistant (Mohs 6–7), and handles hot pans directly from the stove. Granite requires periodic sealing about once every 1–2 years.

Quartz is an engineered stone made from crushed natural quartz crystals and polymer resin. It is non-porous, never needs sealing, and offers a consistent color and pattern across slabs.

Both are great choices. You can compare all materials side by side in our Stone Comparison Guide.

Natural marble (such as Carrara, Calacatta, and Statuario) is unmatched for classic beauty and baking, though it is softer (Mohs 3–4) and more sensitive to acidic foods like lemon juice or vinegar than granite or quartzite. For Utah kitchens, Howard frequently recommends a honed (matte) finish on marble, which conceals everyday etching and develops a warm, velvety patina over time.

Polished and honed marble are also top choices for master bathroom vanities, tub decks, and bookmatched fireplace surrounds. Read our full Marble Countertops Utah Guide →
