Local Expertise Since 1984

Custom Stone Countertops Across the Wasatch Front & Mountain Communities

From historic Salt Lake bungalows to Silicon Slopes new builds and Deer Valley ski estates, we tailor every slab to the architecture of your home.

Salt Lake City, Millcreek & Holladay

In Salt Lake City's historic avenues, Sugar House bungalows, and Millcreek and Holladay custom remodels, we frequently template older, non-square walls and custom cabinetry. Howard also meets homeowners directly at Salt Lake County's major stone wholesale yards to hand-pick honed Carrara marble, granite, and quartzite slabs in person.

Draper, Sandy & South Jordan

South Valley kitchens in Draper, Sandy, Cottonwood Heights, South Jordan, and Herriman often feature expansive 10-foot islands that call for bookmatched Taj Mahal quartzite, Calacatta marble waterfall edges, or durable White Ice granite. We provide free in-home measurements throughout the South Valley.

Alpine, Highland & Lehi

North Utah County blends custom luxury homes in Alpine and Highland with fast-growing neighborhoods in Lehi, Saratoga Springs, and Eagle Mountain. Whether you are installing multi-slab natural stone in an Alpine custom home or zero-maintenance engineered quartz in a busy family kitchen, Howard handles every cut in-house.

Provo, Orem & Central Utah County

Howard has fabricated and installed stone across Provo, Orem, Pleasant Grove, Lindon, Springville, Mapleton, and Spanish Fork since 1984. Our reputation in Utah County is built on word-of-mouth referrals, fair pricing without showroom markups, and tight seams that last a lifetime.

Park City, Deer Valley & Heber Valley

Howard's mountain credentials include commercial stonework at the five-star St. Regis Hotel at Deer Valley. We bring that exact precision to Summit and Wasatch County residences—fabricating custom stone fireplace surrounds, full-height backsplashes, waterfall islands, and spa vanities.
